Island Warriors Street Outreach Society

Who We Are

Island Warriors Street Outreach Society (IWSO) is a peer-led, Indigenous-led grassroots nonprofit organization based in Victoria, British Columbia.

We are people with lived experience helping people. Our leadership and outreach are grounded in Indigenous values, culture, community, and the understanding that healing begins with connection.

Many of our team members understand firsthand the realities of addiction, homelessness, incarceration, trauma, poverty, and recovery. We take those lived experiences and turn them into purpose—walking alongside people who may be facing the same struggles we once faced ourselves.

We believe that lived experience is lived expertise.

Our approach is built around compassion, dignity, respect, cultural connection, and meeting people exactly where they are. We don't look down on people or judge them for where they are in their journey.

We sit beside them. We listen. We build trust. And when they are ready, we walk with them.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide peer-led, Indigenous-led outreach, support, advocacy, cultural connection, and recovery navigation to people impacted by homelessness, addiction, trauma, incarceration, poverty, and social isolation.

We work to bridge the gaps between people on the street and the services, community connections, cultural supports, and recovery opportunities available to them.

Through lived experience and Indigenous teachings, we strive to restore hope, dignity, identity, belonging, and connection.

Our mission is simple:

Meet people where they are and walk beside them toward where they want to be.

Our Vision

Our vision is a community where no one is forgotten and no one has to walk alone.

We envision Indigenous people and all community members having access to culturally safe, peer-led support that recognizes the whole person—not simply their addiction, homelessness, criminal history, or circumstances.

We want to see people with lived experience become leaders, mentors, advocates, and examples that change is possible.

Our vision goes beyond helping someone survive another night.

We want to help people reconnect with culture, community, family, identity, purpose, recovery, and hope.

What We Do

Island Warriors Street Outreach provides:

- Peer-Led Street Outreach — Meeting people directly where they are with food, clothing, water, hygiene supplies, seasonal necessities, compassion, and connection.
- Indigenous-Led Cultural Support — Connecting people with culture, teachings, ceremony, drumming, circles, Elders, Knowledge Keepers, and culturally grounded opportunities for healing.
- Peer Support — One-on-one support from people who understand addiction, homelessness, incarceration, trauma, and recovery through lived experience.
- Recovery Navigation — Helping people explore detox, treatment, recovery programs, meetings, cultural healing, and other pathways toward recovery.
- Advocacy & Systems Navigation — Helping people understand and navigate services while advocating for dignity, respect, and fair treatment.
- Referrals & Community Connections — Connecting people with healthcare, Indigenous services, recovery resources, community organizations, and other available supports.
- Community Outreach & Events — Community feeds, cultural activities, peer-led circles, donation drives, workshops, and events that create connection.
- Partnership Building — Working with Indigenous communities, nonprofits, service providers, businesses, governments, volunteers, and community members to help close gaps in services.

All My Relations

All My Relations is more than something we say—it reflects how we try to walk in this world.

It reminds us that we are connected to one another, our ancestors, future generations, our communities, and the land.

That is why we don't see "the homeless" when we walk onto the street.

We see our relatives.

Someone's son. Someone's daughter. A mother, father, brother, sister, auntie, uncle, Elder, friend, or loved one.

Our doors and our hearts are open to people from all walks of life. Indigenous-led does not mean Indigenous-only. It means our work is guided by Indigenous values of relationship, responsibility, respect, reciprocity, and caring for one another.

Our Commitment

We believe connection creates hope, and hope creates the possibility for change.

Island Warriors Street Outreach is committed to showing up, building relationships, listening without judgment, advocating for our relatives, and helping people find pathways toward healing.

We cannot fix everything for someone.

But we can make sure they know they are seen, heard, valued, and not alone.
