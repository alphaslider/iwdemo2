Meet Angela Wauthy

Vice President & Outreach Coordinator
Island Warriors Street Outreach Society

My name is Angela Wauthy, and I am proud to serve as the Vice President and Outreach Coordinator of Island Warriors Street Outreach Society.

My journey hasn't always been easy. I have lived experience with addiction and homelessness, and I know firsthand what it feels like to struggle, to face judgment, and to experience some of life's darkest moments.

But I also know that change is possible.

Through my own experiences, I have developed a deep understanding and compassion for those who are struggling. I've worked as an outreach worker, supporting individuals facing homelessness, addiction, trauma, and other barriers.

Today, I use my past not as something that defines me, but as something that gives me purpose.

Being part of Island Warriors means more than just helping people. It means standing beside our unhoused relatives, listening without judgment, offering hope, and reminding people that they matter.

I believe everyone deserves dignity, respect, compassion, and an opportunity to build a better life. Sometimes all it takes is one person believing in you when you've stopped believing in yourself.

As an Indigenous-led, peer-driven organization, Island Warriors is about building connections, restoring hope, and bringing our communities together.

My past gave me experience. My journey gave me strength. And today, my purpose is to use both to help others.

I am proud to stand alongside our team and continue bringing love, compassion, and hope to the streets of Victoria.

All My Relations.
Angela Wauthy
Vice President & Outreach Coordinator
